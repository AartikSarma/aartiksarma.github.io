Short bio, for introductions

Aartik Sarma, MD, MAS is an Assistant Professor of Medicine at UCSF and cares for patients in the ICUs at UCSF Parnassus. He is core faculty at the UCSF Center for Precision Research in Critical Care and Affiliate Faculty in the UCSF/UC Berkeley Joint Program in Computational Precision Health. His NIH-funded research uses computational tools — from RNA sequencing to LLMs — to find better treatments for critical illness, and spans preclinical models, observational cohorts, and clinical trials. He is an investigator in the NHLBI ARDS, Pneumonia, and Sepsis Consortium and the Common Longitudinal ICU Format Consortium, and a recipient of the ASCI Emerging Generation Award.
