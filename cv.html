Short bio, for introductions

Aartik Sarma is a pulmonary and critical care physician at UCSF. He cares for patients in the intensive care unit and runs a research program that sequences RNA from the lungs of critically ill patients to find the biological distinctions that current definitions of critical illness miss. He also builds open tools for working with intensive care data across hospitals.
